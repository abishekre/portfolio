Quote requests are saved with a lead status, emailed through Resend and pinged into the team's Discord, so nobody has to watch an inbox. Most enquiries come from phones, so there's a WhatsApp button and a mobile action bar.

The admin side has a Photo studio that previews a phone photo in the site's warm styling and gives tips before it's uploaded.
