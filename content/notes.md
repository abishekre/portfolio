# hi,

I'm Abishek, an independent developer from Kerala.

I build apps around how people here actually live: bank SMS and UPI, Malayalam, a sports hall with bad wifi, ten friends splitting one dinner bill. Most of them work offline, keep your data on your phone, and come with more tests than they strictly need.

Everything on this phone is something I designed and built myself, except Dazzle Designs, which I made for a client.

## How this site was made

It's an Astro site pretending to be a phone. Each app opens in its own colours and fonts, taken from the real app. The clock is Kochi time, and after 7 pm the wallpaper goes lamp-lit.

On a phone there's no frame: the page is the home screen, and Back or the Home button closes an app.

On a computer, press and hold any icon on the home screen. You'll find out I couldn't resist.
